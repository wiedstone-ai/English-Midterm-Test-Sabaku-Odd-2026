Kelas X — ASAS Gasal Penuh (Fase E)

Latihan Asesmen Sumatif Akhir Semester (ASAS) Gasal

Satu Semester Penuh Gasal (18 Minggu — 40 Soal):
• 10 Soal Listening Komprehensif (Pictures, Talks, Fauna)
• TP 10.1: Descriptive Text (Karimunjawa & Anthony Sinisuka Ginting)
• TP 10.2: Scientific Report (Orangutan & Panas Bumi Dieng)
• Grammar & Kosakata: Passive Voice Ilmiah, OSASCOMP, Conditionals, Relative Pronoun (whose), Oxford 3000 Level A2/B1
• Komposisi: 60% (24 Soal) Bank Soal ASAS Gasal + 40% (16 Soal) Komplementer Penuh

Kelas XI — ASAS Gasal Penuh

Latihan ASAS (Akhir Semester)

• Reading Genre 2: Analytical Exposition (Critical Digital Literacy)
• Grammar: Past Perfect, Reported Speech, Conditionals Type 2
• Kosakata: Oxford 3000 Level B1/B2

Kelas XI — Tingkat Lanjut (TL)

Latihan ASAS B. Inggris Tingkat Lanjut

Satu Semester Penuh (Fase F — TL):
• 10 Soal Listening (Digital Humanities, Classic Theatre, Climate Observatory)
• Genre 1: Advanced Narrative (The Gilded Weaver of Mount Merapi)
• Genre 2: Hortatory Exposition (Ethical AI in High Schools)
• Grammar: Negative Inversion, Subjunctive Mood, Concession
• Kosakata: Oxford 5000 Level B2

Mulai Latihan ASAS Tingkat Lanjut XI ➔
Kelas XII — ASAS Gasal Penuh (Fase F)

Latihan Asesmen Sumatif Akhir Semester (ASAS) Gasal

• 10 Soal Listening (Academic Debate, Solar/Wind Facility, IoT Greenhouse, & Keynote Talks)
• TP 12.1: Discussion Text (Generative AI in Education & Nuclear vs Renewables in Indonesia)
• TP 12.2: Review Text (Andrea Hirata's "The Rainbow Troops" & Smart IoT Agriculture)
